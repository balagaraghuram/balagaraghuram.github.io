Who is Balaga Raghuram?

Balaga Raghuram is a Data Scientist at NHS England and an AI/ML researcher based in the United Kingdom. He works on AI-driven oncology research and clinical prediction modelling, and previously built machine learning solutions for UAE real estate analytics. He is an alumnus of the University of Birmingham and has attended conferences and funded winter schools at the University of Cambridge, University of Oxford, University of Edinburgh, and Lancaster University.
